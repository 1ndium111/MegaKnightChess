📜 Mega-Knight Chess Rules

1. The 2x2 Mega Knight

A massive, neutral crimson Mega Knight occupies the center four squares (d4, d5, e4, e5). It is neutral and can be moved by either player!

2. Turn Structure & The D6 Dice

At the start of your turn, you have two options:

  • Make a standard chess move with one of your pieces, OR
  • Roll the D6 to command the Mega Knight.

To move the Mega Knight, you must roll a 6! If you roll 1-5, your turn ends immediately.

3. Vertex Movement & Crushing

The Mega Knight travels along the vertices (grid intersections). It moves in an L-shape: 2 vertices along a grid line, then 1 vertex perpendicular!

When the Mega Knight lands, any pieces occupying the 4 squares are crushed and permanently removed from the board!

⚠️ Crushmate: This is true even for Kings! Crushing the enemy King results in an instant checkmate victory!

4. Instant Mega Knight Summoning

If four knights (White, Black, or collaborating!) occupy 4 squares in a 2x2 square pattern through promotion or maneuvers, another Mega Knight will form instantaneously on the board! There is no limit to how many Mega Knights can form!
